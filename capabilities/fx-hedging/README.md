# FX Hedging Capability

## Overview

This capability evaluates foreign exchange transaction risk and compares four strategies: no hedge, a forward contract, a money-market hedge, and a put option. The goal is to help companies manage exchange-rate uncertainty and protect their future cash flows.

## Application — Scenario 9: Laboratory Instruments

This capability is applied to a U.S. manufacturer of laboratory mass spectrometers expecting a €5,100,000 payment from an Austrian research institute in 182 days. Because the receivable is denominated in euros, depreciation against the U.S. dollar could reduce the company's realized USD proceeds.

The model will compare the four strategies under different settlement exchange rates to evaluate cash-flow certainty, downside protection, and hedging costs.
