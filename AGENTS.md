
# AI Working Conventions

## About Me
I am a Finance and Marketing double major at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, Shidler College of Business. I expect to graduate in Spring 2027.

My interests include marketing research, financial analysis, data analysis, and business strategy.

## How I Prefer to Work
- Use clear and simple English.
- Keep explanations professional but easy to understand.
- Explain difficult concepts step by step.
- Use examples when explaining finance or marketing concepts.
- Keep my writing style natural and avoid overly complicated words.
- Ask questions when instructions are unclear.
- Give feedback on my work and explain how I can improve it.

## Academic Integrity
- Follow my course's AI-use rules.
- Let me write the first draft of my academic work.
- AI may help with research organization, calculations, technical setup, and feedback when permitted.
- Do not invent sources, citations, research findings, or data.
- Clearly identify assumptions and information that needs verification.
- Keep a record of meaningful AI assistance in prompt-log.md.

## Privacy and Security
Never include the following in public files or AI prompts:
- Passwords, authentication codes, or API keys.
- Bank account or financial account information.
- My home address or personal phone number.
- Private family or medical information.
- Confidential employer or customer information.
- Private student records or information about other people.
- Any files containing sensitive personal information.

## Project Organization
- Organize projects by capability and engagement, not by course or week.
- Store project specifications in capabilities/.
- Store project briefs in docs/briefs/.
- Store decisions and recommendations in docs/decisions/.
- Store findings and charts in analysis/.
- Store sourced data with provenance in data/.
- Use models/ only for Performance Ratios work.
- Use descriptive file names.

## Quality Checks
- Check calculations and formulas before presenting results.
- Verify sources and citations.
- Do not present guesses as facts.
- Review files before committing and pushing.
- Keep work organized and easy for others to understand.

## Prompt Log
At the end of meaningful AI-assisted work sessions, prepare an entry for prompt-log.md.

Each entry should include:
- Date
- Task or goal
- AI tool used
- What AI helped with
- What I checked or changed
- Any errors or limitations

Do not invent or backfill AI sessions. Ask for confirmation before adding an entry.
